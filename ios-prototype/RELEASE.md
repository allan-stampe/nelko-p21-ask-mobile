Experimental **iOS BLE hardware-test prototype**, separate from Android v0.0.3.

The IPA is **unsigned**: use a local Windows sideloading tool such as [Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io/) to sign/install with your own Apple account. A free account normally expires after seven days; no paid membership is required to attempt this route. Follow [the installation/test guide](https://github.com/allan-stampe/nelko-p21-ask-mobile/blob/ios-v0.0.1/ios-prototype/README.md). Never send Apple credentials to chat or GitHub.

Includes BLE scan/connect, battery test, Cancel Pause, Windows-reference bitmap test print, Danish/English UI, UTC ASCII/HEX diagnostics and export. No custom-text designer yet. Close Classic SPP clients before BLE discovery.

**Physical iPhone installation, Bluetooth and printing are NOT confirmed.** Simulator/build tests do not establish hardware compatibility. User reported iPhone 11 Pro / iOS 27.2.1; test installation first, then BLE and one label. Do not automatically repeat failed print jobs.

App use is offline, without app account, cloud, advertising, subscriptions or telemetry. External Apple signing/renewal needs account/network. Android source/version and Windows project remain unchanged. MIT license and original Tyler C credit preserved.

Your printer. Your labels. Your data.  
No cloud. No account. Just print.
