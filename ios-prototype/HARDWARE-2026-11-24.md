# Physical iPhone test — 2026-11-24

Owner: iPhone 11 Pro, reported iOS 27.2.1. iOS test app 0.0.1 was signed/installed using Sideloadly on Windows. The owner confirmed launch and provided a diagnostic log; later reported that the printer beeps but does not print. Identifiers from the private log are deliberately omitted here.

## Observed facts

- Physical installation and launch confirmed by owner.
- iPhone connected; services `FF00, AE3A, AE00` discovered. FF02 write-without-response, FF01/FF03 notify, FF05 indicate; FF04 and FF10 also observed.
- Initial FF03 frames `01 07` and `02 7F 00`. No replenishment frames appear before either timeout.
- Battery command FF02 `42 41 54 54 45 52 59 3F 0D 0A` received an FF01 reply `42 41 54 54 45 52 59 20 99 00 0D 0A`: parser decodes reported level 99%, charging false. This confirms bidirectional BLE communication and matching battery framing, not independent battery calibration.
- First session: one battery packet; then Cancel Pause, SIZE in two packets, GAP, DIRECTION, DENSITY: **seven total packets**. No CLS, BITMAP, raster or PRINT follows. Write times out after 12 seconds.
- Second session: Cancel Pause, SIZE in two packets, GAP, DIRECTION, DENSITY, CLS: **seven total packets**. No BITMAP, raster or PRINT follows. Same timeout.
- **No physical print is confirmed.** A beep alone does not establish successful job reception.

## Code-level cause and correction

0.0.1 consumed the initial seven vendor credits locally for every FF02 packet and refused further writes without a new FF03 grant. That gate explains the deterministic local stall in both sessions. The log does not explain why the vendor sends no replenishment, nor prove the full proprietary flow-control protocol.

0.0.2 uses paced FF02 writes, preserving <=20-byte payloads, >=12ms intervals, CoreBluetooth readiness/backpressure and reported smaller payload limits. FF03 remains diagnostic, not a permission gate. No fake credits, automatic retries or altered TSPL bytes. Published upstream documentation independently reports this paced mode working without credit management; its code was not copied.

Regression tests replay the exact setup reports without replenishment and require packet planning through the complete Windows golden stream, including final PRINT. Apple backpressure must still stop sends. These software checks do not prove radio delivery or printing.
